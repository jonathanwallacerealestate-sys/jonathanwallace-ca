About this home

Why this one stands out

If you've been hoping for a newer custom home with room to breathe, this one is worth a look. It's a bungalow built in the last few years, with 9 ft ceilings, oversized windows that pull in a ton of natural light, and quality finishes throughout.

The main level is open and easy to live in. The kitchen is made for hosting, with a big island, a farmhouse sink, a gas range, and open shelving with a wine rack. The living room has a stone fireplace and custom built-ins, and the dining area sits right beside the kitchen, so nobody gets left out of the conversation.

The lower level gives you a lot of options. There are already two bedrooms down there, plus rough-ins for a future kitchen and bathroom, with the toilet already in. It would make a great in-law suite, or a place to spread out for movie nights and games.

Outside is where this place really shines. The lot is beautifully landscaped and private, with a saltwater inground pool, a fireside spot for evenings, and a covered back patio that feels like an extra room. The 1,000 sq. ft. detached shop has space for the boat, the trailer, the toys, or the workshop you've always wanted.

There's parking for 17, so there's plenty of room when family and friends come to visit. 2,595 sq. ft. above grade plus a partially finished lower level.
