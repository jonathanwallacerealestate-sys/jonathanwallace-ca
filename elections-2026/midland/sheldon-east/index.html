Priorities

Source: Candidate's own website or campaign materials. Up to five points.

  1. Transportation and transit: extend routes, exploring service to the Aberdeen Street and Sunnyside areas and possibly toward Midland Point Road
  2. Affordable housing: work with the County of Simcoe and partners on practical solutions without unnecessary pressure on taxpayers
  3. Community safety: work with Council, the County, the OPP and community organizations on practical improvements
  4. Responsible budgeting that limits pressure on taxpayers while planning for costs that cannot be predicted
  5. Responsible growth, with continued community input on the future of Midland Bay Landing and support for a stronger downtown
