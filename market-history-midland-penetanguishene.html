Midland and Penetanguishene Real Estate Market History: Home Sales and Prices, 2021 to 2026

Market history·Updated with October 2026 figures·~4 min read

By Jonathan Wallace, REALTOR® · Updated with October 2026 figures

Midland and Penetanguishene recorded 391 home sales in 2025 at an average sale price of $579,953, down from 928 sales in 2021. Through October 2026, 314 homes had sold at an average of $563,772. In October 2026 the market had 7.15 months of inventory, which makes it a buyer's market.

The board reports Midland and Penetanguishene together as one market. These figures cover both towns. Full years are January to December. 2026 is January to October, the latest month reported.

For 2021 to 2024, the board's year-end figures for this market include sales and new listings only. Average price, days on market and list to sale ratio are reported from 2025 on, so earlier years show as not reported. They are not estimated.

What changed over five years

Sales in Midland and Penetanguishene peaked in 2021 at 928 and fell to 391 in 2025, a drop of 57.9 percent. Sellers did not leave in the same numbers. New listings were 1,180 in 2021 and 1,206 in 2025, so far more of the homes that come to market now compete for each buyer.

The slide was not a straight line. 2024 sold 595 homes, more than the 556 of 2023, before 2025 fell back hard. Through October 2026 the market had 314 sales, against 331 over the same ten months of 2025.

Common questions about the Midland and Penetanguishene market

How many homes sold in Midland and Penetanguishene in 2025?

391 homes sold in Midland and Penetanguishene in 2025, compared with 595 in 2024 and 928 in 2021.

What is the average home price in Midland and Penetanguishene?

The average sale price was $579,953 for 2025 and $563,772 for January to October 2026. The board did not report a year-end average price for this market before 2025. An average reflects which homes sold, not the value of any one home.

Is Midland and Penetanguishene a buyer's market right now?

In October 2026, Midland and Penetanguishene had 7.15 months of inventory, which is a buyer's market. Above six months is a buyer's market.

How long do homes take to sell in Midland and Penetanguishene?

Homes that sold in 2025 averaged 44 days on market. In October 2026 the average was 44 days. When a listing is cancelled and put back on the market, the days on market count starts over, so some homes have been for sale longer than the number shows.

Are home sales in Midland and Penetanguishene up or down in 2026?

Through October 2026, 314 homes sold, compared with 331 over the same ten months of 2025, down 5.1 percent.
