Getting ready to sell in spring

Before the photographer arrives

By Jonathan Wallace, Realtor with Faris Team Real Estate Brokerage

What's my home worth?
Lights on, blinds open, counters clear, cars out of the driveway.

Before the photographer arrives

  • Every light on, every bulb the same colour
  • Blinds open, windows clean
  • Counters, fridge door and bathroom vanities cleared
  • Cars out of the driveway, garbage bins out of sight
  • Beds made, towels folded, toilet seats down
  • Pets and pet bowls out of the shot
  • Snow cleared from walkways and the deck if it's still around

Interior photos on a bright day, paired with your fall exteriors.
