Should I take my house off the market for the winter?

Jonathan Wallace, REALTOR® · 1:06 video

The short answer

Think twice before you do. Every fall a lot of sellers pull their homes, but buyers keep driving up from the city, so with fewer homes to choose from, yours gets noticed. Stay on through the first cold snap, maybe the second, because that is when people really change what they are doing. If things slow down and you truly believe taking it off is right, take it off, but don't step away while buyers are still looking.

What Jonathan Wallace said

Are you thinking about taking your home off the market today? Well, you should think twice before you do it. It's a beautiful day today. It's going to be 21 degrees. So before you call your realtor and say, let's try it again in the spring, hear me out.

And I get it. The last three winters up here have been really hard, and nobody wants to show a house in a snowstorm. So calling your realtor and saying, let's try again in the spring, sounds pretty good. But here's what happens every fall. Right around now, lots of sellers pull their homes from the market. Meanwhile, buyers keep driving up from the city. Everybody's out enjoying the warm weather, and the long-range forecasts are calling for a mild winter. So with fewer homes to choose from, yours gets noticed.

That's why I'd suggest you hang in there just a little bit longer. Stay on through the first cold snap, maybe the second. That's when people really change what they're doing. And if things slow down and you truly believe taking the home off the market is right, then take it off. But don't step away while buyers are still looking. If you're thinking about pulling your home for winter, think twice. Send me a DM if you want more information.
