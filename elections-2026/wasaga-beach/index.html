Questions

When is Election Day?

Election Day is Monday, October 26, 2026. Voting closes at 8 p.m.

How do I vote in Wasaga Beach?

You can vote online, or by paper ballot in person. The ballot also asks if you are in favour of moving to a ward system, and the answers are Yes or No. Town of Wasaga Beach engagement page.

When can I vote online or by phone?

Voting runs from Thursday, October 1 at 10 a.m. to Monday, October 26 at 8 p.m.

How do I check I'm on the voters' list?

The Town's election page explains how to confirm you are on the list or correct your information. Check you are registered.

What do I need to vote?

You need to be on the voters' list. You can vote online, or by paper ballot at an advance poll or on Election Day.

Where do I get help?

Town of Wasaga Beach's election page has voter help and who to contact. Official election page.
