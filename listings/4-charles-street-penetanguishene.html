MLS® description

In the listing's words

Top 5 Reasons You Will Love This Home:

1) Beautifully maintained all-brick raised bungalow showcasing inviting curb appeal and a fully landscaped property, with a walkout leading to a private backyard designed for relaxing and entertaining

2) Enjoy the convenience of main level living with two bedrooms, two bathrooms, and laundry all on one floor, complemented by an attached double-car garage with direct interior access

3) Extend your living space into the finished lower level featuring a spacious family room with a cozy gas fireplace, an office, two additional bedrooms, and a separate entrance providing excellent potential for extended family or in-law living

4) Set within a welcoming neighbourhood enjoyed by both families and retirees, the home is conveniently located close to downtown, shopping, golf courses, healthcare, and exceptional deep-water boating opportunities

5) Take advantage of nearby parks, schools, a marina, library, and public transit, along with access to a community centre through an optional association fee, providing plenty of opportunities for recreation and connection close to home. 1,152 above grade sq.ft. plus a finished lower level.
