In the listing's words

Top 5 Reasons You Will Love This Home:

1) More than just a water view, this home offers a true waterfront experience where the water becomes your everyday backdrop, with every room oriented toward the sandy shoreline and sparkling Georgian Bay, creating a seamless connection to sunrises, calm afternoons, and glowing evening skies

2) The covered waterside porch serves as a natural extension of the living space, perfect for enjoying shaded midday relaxation, watching summer storms roll across the Bay, or unwinding into peaceful evenings with uninterrupted shoreline views

3) Designed for the way waterfront living is meant to be enjoyed, the property features a private dock, boat lift, and Seadoo lift, allowing for effortless days on the water, while the professionally graded shoreline flows naturally to the sandy beach for a clean, low-maintenance, and highly functional outdoor setting

4) Inside, a spacious and functional kitchen anchors the home for both everyday living and entertaining, complemented by an oversized garage with potential for additional living space above, and a bright extension with soaring ceilings offering flexibility for a guest suite, games room, or additional family area

5) Set among thoughtfully spaced neighbouring properties, the home offers a rare sense of privacy, enhanced by major updates including mechanicals, windows, doors, and deck, all while being just minutes from downtown Penetanguishene with convenient access to shops, restaurants, and essential amenities. 1,618 above grade sq.ft.
