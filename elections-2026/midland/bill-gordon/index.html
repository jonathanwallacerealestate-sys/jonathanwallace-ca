Candidate, Town of Midland

Bill Gordon

Candidate for MayorTown of Midland1 seat, elected at largeCurrent Mayor

Mayor since 2022 and a councillor from 2018 to 2022, according to his website, seeking a second term. His platform points to more than $30 million in grants and non-tax revenue, more than 2,000 homes approved and a 0.98 per cent core tax increase for 2026.

Priorities

Source: Candidate's own website or campaign materials. Up to five points.

  1. Controlling spending: separate wants from needs, no deficit budgets, and press the Province to fix Municipal Partnership Funding
  2. Housing: turn the 2,000-plus approved homes into homes built, and donate Town land on Bay Street to the County of Simcoe for roughly 100 affordable seniors' units
  3. Community safety: routine OPP foot patrols downtown and in-town radar enforcement, more Neighbourhood Watch programs, expanded bylaw patrols and public safety cameras downtown and in parks
  4. Midland Bay Landing: re-engage the community to review the master plan before any next step
  5. Shared services with Tiny, Tay and Penetanguishene, plus parkland preservation, infrastructure repair and climate resiliency
