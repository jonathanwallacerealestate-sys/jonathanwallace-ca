How do I buy my next home before I sell my current one?

Jonathan Wallace, REALTOR® · 1:08 video

The short answer

Make your offer conditional on selling your house by a set date, and add a pause clause. Without it, the seller can keep marketing their home and give you 24 to 48 hours to commit the moment another offer comes in, before your own sale is firm. The pause clause means that once you accept an offer on your home, the seller has to wait and can't bump you out, so your buyer commits, then you commit.

What Jonathan Wallace said

Need to sell your house before you can buy the next one? There's a way to do it without ending up homeless. Usually you make an offer on the new house, and it only goes ahead if you sell your house by a certain date. If it doesn't sell in time, the deal ends and you get your deposit back. Now, the catch is the seller gets to keep their house on the market. If another offer comes in, you get 24, maybe 48 hours to commit, or you lose it.

So picture a couple who finds the house that they've been waiting for. The seller says yes, and they list their home. A few weeks later they get an offer on their place, but that buyer still needs an inspection and bank approval. That same week, someone else comes and offers on the house they want. Now they only have 24 to 48 hours to commit to a house they can't afford yet. They could sell their home and have nowhere to go.

That's why we add the pause clause. Once you accept an offer for your home, the seller has to wait the duration of the pause clause, and they can't bump you out. Your buyer commits, then you commit, and you move into the house you wanted. So if you need to sell before you buy, send me a DM and I'll make sure you're protected.
