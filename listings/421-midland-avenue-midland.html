About this home

Why this one stands out

Some houses feel like home the minute you step onto the porch. This century home dates back to 1900, and it still has the covered front porch, the original character, and the big principal rooms that make older homes so easy to live in. You can tell it's been well cared for.

The location is a big part of the appeal. Little Lake Park, the Midland Curling Club, the North Simcoe Sports and Recreation Centre, the YMCA, downtown shops and cafés, Midland Harbour, and the Georgian Bay waterfront trails are all within easy reach.

Along with three bedrooms and a finished basement, there's an unfinished third level waiting for whatever you need: a family room, an office, a studio, a playroom, or a big primary suite.

Outside, you'll find established gardens, mature landscaping, a lovely walkway, a private backyard, and a good-sized deck for summer dinners and slow mornings.

The big updates are done: new furnace (2026), water softener (2025), roof (2024), central air (2023), windows (2019), and upgraded 200-amp electrical. Add the attached garage and double driveway, and everyday life gets a lot easier. 1,811 sq. ft. above grade plus a finished basement.
