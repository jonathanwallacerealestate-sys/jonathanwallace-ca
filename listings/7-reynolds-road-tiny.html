About this home

Why this one stands out

If you need room for everyone, this one deserves a look. It's in one of the most sought-after pockets of Wyevale, with six bedrooms, five bathrooms, nearly 3,000 sq. ft. on the main level, and 5,771 sq. ft. of finished space in total. The 1,200 sq. ft. heated garage is a big bonus, whether it holds vehicles, a workshop, or all the gear.

The primary suite is a real retreat. There's a big walk-in closet and an ensuite with a dual sink vanity, a large walk-in shower, a corner jetted soaker tub, and a private water closet. You can also step straight out to your own private deck for some fresh air.

Coffered ceilings dress up the living room and the primary bedroom. There's a future TV room with built-ins already framed and ready for your ideas, roomy guest bedrooms, and a butler's pantry with a second dishwasher, a full fridge, and loads of storage. If you like to host, you'll love that pantry.

Downstairs is fully finished, with three more bedrooms, an exercise room, and a huge rec room for movie nights and game days. In-floor heating and an ICF foundation keep it comfortable and energy efficient.

Out back, the yard is nicely landscaped, with a new fence for privacy. You're steps from Wyevale School, Jug City, parks, and the Tiny walking trail. Bluewater Beach is a short drive, and Barrie is about 25 minutes away. 2,980 sq. ft. above grade plus a finished basement.
