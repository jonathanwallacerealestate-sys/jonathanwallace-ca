Low-Cost Ways to Give Back in Midland, Penetang, Tiny, Tay and Wasaga Beach This December

By Jonathan Wallace, REALTOR®, Faris Team Real Estate Brokerage · November 27, 2026 · ~5 min read

The short answer: December gets expensive fast. Giving back doesn't have to. Most of what helps families around the Bay this month costs a couple of hours or a bag of groceries, and all of it stays in Midland, Penetanguishene, Tiny, Tay and Wasaga Beach. Here is where to start, step by step.

Key takeaways
  • Two hours is enough: kettle shifts, toy sorting, food bank packing and a Monday lunch in Penetang all need help.
  • One new toy or a bag of non-perishable food counts. Drop boxes are open in Midland, Penetang, Waubaushene and Wasaga Beach until December 20.
  • Midland lets you pay an eligible parking ticket with a toy or food until December 4.
  • Every listing here was checked against an official 2026 source.

Give a Couple of Hours This December

Volunteering is the lowest-cost way to help. Pick one shift, show up, and you have made a difference.

Salvation Army Midland

555 Dominion Ave., Midland. Two-hour Christmas kettle shifts run through December 24, and there are also roles in the food bank, the community kitchen and the Christmas toy store. The local goal is $80,000, and the money stays in Midland. Apply to volunteer or call 705-526-5683.

Simcoe Muskoka Family Connexions holiday program

741 Yonge St., Units 7 and 8, Midland. December only, Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Sort toys, pack hampers and help with deliveries. Email holiday@familyconnexions.ca or call 705-984-5184. Program details.

Georgian Bay Food Network

230 Aberdeen Blvd., Units 1 and 2, Midland. Unpack deliveries, stock shelves and help shoppers, Tuesday to Thursday, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. Email admin@gbfn.org or call 705-812-6500. gbfn.org.

Wasaga Beach Ministerial Food Bank

818 Mosley St., Unit 2, Wasaga Beach. Packing, sorting, intake and deliveries. The greatest need is Tuesday and Thursday, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m., and Thursday, 6 to 8:30 p.m. Email vc@wasagabeachfoodbank.ca. Volunteer with the food bank.

Caring Kitchen, Penetanguishene

First Presbyterian Church, 20 Robert St. E., Penetanguishene. A free lunch every Monday, 11:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. Help with prep, serving or greeting. Call Mary Sheriff in the evenings at 705-526-7591.

Drop Off a Toy or a Bag of Groceries

If you have ten minutes and a few dollars, these drop-offs make it easy.

  1. County of Simcoe Paramedics Toy and Food Drive, until December 20. New unwrapped toys, non-perishable food, gift cards or money. Drop boxes at 395 Cranston Cres., Midland; 10 Robert St. W., Penetanguishene; 415 Sturgeon Bay Rd., Waubaushene; and 23 Oxbow Park Rd., Wasaga Beach. Pop-ups on December 5 at the Midland Walmart, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., and December 12 at the Wasaga Walmart, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Details.
  2. Wasaga Kinettes Toys for Kids, unwrapped toys until December 18 at Canadian Tire, Superstore, Town Hall, the YMCA, both fire halls, IDA, River Road Family Dental and Beach Chiropractic. You can also e-transfer kinettestoysforkids@gmail.com (answer: donation).
  3. CTV Barrie Toy Mountain for Family Connexions, new toys until December 18, especially teen gifts and gift cards. Drop them at Access Storage, 729 Balm Beach Rd. E. or 679 Prospect Blvd., Midland. Check that a bin is on site. Details.
  4. Tiny Township Lions Breakfast with Santa, Saturday, December 5, 9 to 11 a.m., Wyevale United Church, 846 County Rd. 6 S. Free with a non-perishable donation for the Elmvale and District Food Bank. tinylions.ca.
  5. St. Vincent de Paul Tay food bank, 266 Albert St., Victoria Harbour. Non-perishables accepted Wednesday and Friday, 10 to 11:30 a.m. 705-956-0022.
  6. Georgian Bay Food Network and the Wasaga Beach food bank also take food donations during the hours listed above.

Pay a Midland Parking Ticket With a Toy

Got a parking ticket in Midland since November 1? Under Toys for Tickets, eligible tickets issued November 1 to December 4 can be paid with new unwrapped toys or food equal to the fine. Bring the receipt to Town Hall, 575 Dominion Ave., by December 4. Everything goes to the Salvation Army Midland. Program details.

Fill a Bag for the Hospice Huronia Wish List

Tomkins House, the hospice home run by Hospice Huronia, has posted its Christmas wish list. These are everyday items that keep the house running for residents and their families. Pick up one or two things on your next grocery run.

Drop off: 948 Fuller Ave., Penetanguishene (at Robillard Dr.). The office is open Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Call 705-549-1034 before you go. hospicehuronia.ca.

Gift cards

  • Grocery stores, hardware and home goods

Drinks

  • Pop and bottled water
  • Coffee pods (they go through a lot)
  • Tetley Orange Pekoe tea bags
  • Lynch's Apple Cider and Cranberry Apple Cider packets
  • Hot chocolate packets

Baking and pantry

  • Thompson raisins and sweetened dried cranberries
  • Walnuts and pecans
  • Shredded coconut and baking cocoa
  • Squeezable honey and quick oats
  • Canned ham, tuna, salmon and chicken

Snacks

  • Dad's individually wrapped cookies
  • Individual snacks such as apple sauce and chips

Personal care

  • Razors (triple blade, flexible head)
  • Spray deodorant for men and women
  • Chapstick, body wash and body lotion
  • Kleenex

Around the house

  • Batteries (AA, AAA and D)
  • Soft white facecloths and bath towels
  • Hand casting kits

A Few More Ways to Help

  • Hospice Huronia in Penetanguishene recruits volunteers year-round, and takes wish list donations at Tomkins House (see the list above).
  • Midland Alliance Community Christmas Dinner, December 12, free. Confirm the time with the church before you go.

Giving doesn't have to be big. One toy, one bag of groceries or one two-hour shift changes Christmas morning for a family a few streets over.

Questions People Ask

Where can I volunteer for Christmas in Midland, Ontario?

In December 2026 the Salvation Army Midland, 555 Dominion Ave., needs Christmas kettle volunteers for two-hour shifts through December 24, plus food bank, community kitchen and toy store helpers. Simcoe Muskoka Family Connexions needs weekday volunteers to sort toys and pack hampers at 741 Yonge St., and the Georgian Bay Food Network at 230 Aberdeen Blvd. needs help Tuesday to Thursday, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m.

Where can I drop off toys in Midland and Penetanguishene?

The County of Simcoe Paramedic Services Toy and Food Drive takes new unwrapped toys until December 20, 2026 at 395 Cranston Cres., Midland, 10 Robert St. W., Penetanguishene, 415 Sturgeon Bay Rd., Waubaushene and 23 Oxbow Park Rd., Wasaga Beach. CTV Barrie Toy Mountain also takes new toys until December 18 at Access Storage, 729 Balm Beach Rd. E. and 679 Prospect Blvd., Midland.

How can I volunteer at the Wasaga Beach food bank?

Email vc@wasagabeachfoodbank.ca. The Wasaga Beach Ministerial Food Bank at 818 Mosley St., Unit 2, needs help with packing, sorting, intake and deliveries, with the greatest need Tuesday and Thursday from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. and Thursday from 6 to 8:30 p.m.

Can I pay a Midland parking ticket with a toy?

Yes. Under Midland's Toys for Tickets program, eligible parking tickets issued November 1 to December 4, 2026 can be paid with new unwrapped toys or food equal to the fine, with the receipt, at Town Hall, 575 Dominion Ave., by December 4. Donations go to the Salvation Army Midland.

What are free or low-cost ways to give back this Christmas in North Simcoe?

Give a two-hour volunteer shift, drop one new toy or a bag of non-perishable food at a drop box, or attend the free Tiny Township Lions Breakfast with Santa on December 5, 2026 with a food donation. Every option in this guide costs a few hours or a few dollars, and the help stays in Midland, Penetanguishene, Tiny, Tay and Wasaga Beach.

What does Hospice Huronia need for Christmas?

Tomkins House, Hospice Huronia's hospice home at 948 Fuller Ave., Penetanguishene, is asking for grocery, hardware and home goods gift cards, coffee pods, tea, cider and hot chocolate packets, baking and pantry staples, individual snacks, batteries, soft white towels and facecloths, and personal care items like razors, deodorant, body wash and lotion. Drop off Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., and call 705-549-1034 first.

All details are current as of November 28, 2026 and were checked against each organization's or municipality's own 2026 page. Hours and drop-off sites can change, so check the link before you go.
