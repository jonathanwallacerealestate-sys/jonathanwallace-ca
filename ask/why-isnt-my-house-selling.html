Why isn't my house selling?

Jonathan Wallace, REALTOR® · 1:05 video

The short answer

When the market slows, three things decide whether a home sells: the price, how it looks, and how many buyers see it. One home sat for almost a year, then sold in under 10 days with a new plan: fresh photos, staging, virtual furniture in the empty rooms, a video tour, drone photography showing the beach, and a price that stood out as one of the best deals around.

What Jonathan Wallace said

It sat on the market for almost a year, and then it sold in under 10 days. Same house, different plan. Now, the couple who owned it asked me one thing at their kitchen table: it's a beautiful home, why won't anybody buy it? It was putting their next move on hold, and the wait was costing them tens of thousands of dollars.

When the market slows down, three things decide if a home sells: the price, how it looks, and how many buyers ultimately see the property. Now, their photos were just average, so we started fresh with new photos. We brought in our stager to set up the rooms that already had furniture, and for a few empty rooms we added furniture to those photos digitally, so buyers could see how they could look. Then we put it everywhere buyers look, with a video tour and drone photography showing how close it was to the beach, and we priced it so that it stood out as one of the best deals around.

Under 10 days later it was sold, and they finally got to move on. So if your home's been sitting and no one's told you why, I'm happy to take an honest look.
