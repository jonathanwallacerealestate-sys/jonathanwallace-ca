MLS® description

In the listing's words

Top 5 Reasons You Will Love This Home:

1) With 75' of waterfront and direct access to Georgian Bay, this is true shoreline living, set along a protected channel, your boat stays sheltered from prevailing winds while still enjoying quick access to open water and the kind of sunsets people travel hours to experience, to top it all off, you also have access to two sandy beaches within walking distance

2) Almost half an acre in a rural setting gives you breathing room and long-term value, with no direct neighbours across the street, adding a level of privacy that new subdivisions simply cannot offer, yet you are still within close proximity to Highway 400 for an easy commute

3) The expansive great room serves as the heart of the home, highlighted by oversized windows, a vaulted ceiling, built-in bookshelves, and a fireplace that beautifully anchors the space as natural light pours in, while the primary bedroom offers serene water views so you can wake up to Georgian Bay every day

4) Fully renovated main level bedroom complete with a wet bar and centre island creates the perfect in-law setup or separate overflow living space when guests arrive, along with a main level laundry room for easy convenience

5) Peace of mind comes with the roof, furnace, and air conditioner already replaced, complemented by efficient European windows throughout the main level bedroom addition, as well as four updated windows flanking the fireplace in the great room, while a detached garage and powered auxiliary storage and workshop building provide plenty of space for tools, toys, and projects. 2,602 fin.sq.ft.
