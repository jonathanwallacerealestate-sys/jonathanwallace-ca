Is now a good time to sell my waterfront home on Georgian Bay?

The short answer

From Halloween to mid-November a lot of waterfront homes come off the market, so the ones that stay have far less competition. What Jonathan Wallace said

Is now a good time to sell your waterfront? That's the question I keep hearing at kitchen tables right now. Just today, I was sitting with a family and they asked me, do we sell now or do we wait till spring? Nobody wants to get that one wrong.

But here's the reality. If you're hoping for 2021 prices, that ship has sailed. Even if next year gets a little bit better, it won't be the number that you remember. Right now, roughly one in eight homes are selling. And from Halloween to mid-November, a lot of our waterfront homes come off the market, as owners don't want to shovel snow or keep the place ready for showings all winter. So the homes that do stay on the market have a lot less competition.

Now, could waiting work? Sure, it could. But the real question is, can you afford the risk? If prices drop another 3% by spring, and from what I'm seeing in the economy and home sales, the market is still sliding in the wrong direction. So if I told you waiting could cost you $50,000, would you still wait?

People are still buying homes: new jobs, new chapters, people finally ready for life on the water. Don't stop looking just because it snowed. So before you decide at your own table, let's look at your numbers together. Send me a DM.
