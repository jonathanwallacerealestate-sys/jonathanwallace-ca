North Simcoe Waterfront Market: 2025 vs 2026

By Jonathan Wallace, REALTOR®, Faris Team Real Estate, Brokerage · Updated November 7, 2026

Draft: property records under review.

From January 1 to November 7, this search returned 50 sold waterfront listings in 2026 and 19 in 2025. The median sold price moved from $1,505,000 to $1,200,000, down 20.27%. The average moved from $1,494,684.21 to $1,339,545, down 10.38%.

That is not a 20.27% drop in the value of the same house. Different properties sold, at different prices, with different kinds of water access. Use the town figures and the price bands before you price one home from the four-town number.

You can look up price, how many listings sold, how long the listing was on market, and, once the records are added, browse by town, price, and frontage. The towns are Tiny, Penetanguishene, Midland, and Tay. The price range is $670,000 to $3,000,000.

Town by town

Tiny. 11 sales became 26. The median stayed at $1,600,000. The mean fell 6.19%, from $1,661,454.55 to $1,558,692.31. A steady median and a lower average can happen together when the homes in the set are different.

Penetanguishene. One sale in 2025, at $2,050,000, against five in 2026. The median falls to $707,000, down 65.51%. One sale is too small to treat that percentage as the change in a typical home.

Midland. Four sales became ten. The mean fell 6.74%, from $1,246,250 to $1,162,275. The median fell 21.51%, from $1,290,000 to $1,012,500. The share under $1,500,000 rose from 50% to 70%.

Tay. Three sales became nine. The mean rose 12.36% and the median rose 25.30%, from $838,000 to $1,050,000. Tay moved the other way from the four-town median. That is why the combined number should not set the price of one house.

What the average can and cannot say

More sales at lower prices can pull the overall average down without measuring whether the same homes sold for less. In this search, the share under $1,500,000 rose from 47.37% to 60%. The middle band, $1,500,000 to under $2,300,000, fell from 47.37% to 32%. The top band, $2,300,000 to $3,000,000, rose from 5.26% to 8%.

Lot size, shore length, condition, and the kind of water access were not held the same. This page does not say what caused the mix to change.

Mean listing days on market rose from 44.21 to 52.98. The median rose from 32 to 38. Those are the days on that listing. They are not official cumulative days on market, and they are not the full time a property may have been offered before.

If you are buying, match the water access and the property type, not only the town average. Shared condo water and direct shoreline are not the same thing. If you are selling, a lower four-town median is not a reason to cut one home's price by 20.27%.

What this search includes

As of November 7, 2026. REALM Waterfront Yes and Island No. Freehold and condo. Sold. Tiny, Penetanguishene, Midland, and Tay. Sold price from $670,000 to $3,000,000. Sold dates from January 1 to November 7 in each year, not the closing date.

The set includes direct shoreline, shared waterfront, and indirect access, including condos. It is not only private shoreline, and it is not every mainland waterfront property. A record with an unknown island value is not in this set. Being left out does not prove the property is on an island.

For sold listings, the full 2025 year matches this January 1 to November 7 window. A search from November 8 through December 31, 2025 returned no sales. There is no 2024 chapter. 2026 stops on November 7.

Price bands do not overlap: $670,000 to under $1,500,000, then $1,500,000 to under $2,300,000, then $2,300,000 through $3,000,000. Sold prices are used here. A frontage field of zero is not a measured shore length, so it gets no frontage band. Metres are not converted to feet. A unit conflict is not given a band. Lot width is not water frontage.

Photos and the property-by-property list are not on this page. When they are added, a card can hold up to 10 photos that are actually on file. Missing photos stay missing.

Listings and linked properties

Each listing is one time on the market: sold, expired, or terminated. Linked properties group only a confirmed match. If one of those listings sold, that sale is the outcome. An expired or terminated listing is not, by itself, a home that never sold. An address that only might be the same property stays separate.

Questions

Did waterfront values fall 20.27%?

No. The median of this sold set fell 20.27%. The homes are not the same homes. This is not a same-home price index.

Are the two years the same length?

Yes for this table. Both use January 1 through November 7. The rest of 2025, November 8 through December 31, returned no sales in the same search.

Does waterfront mean you own the shore?

No. The search includes direct, shared, and indirect access. Those are separate filters on the property list.

Why is the property list empty?

The sold totals above are for this search. Individual listing records and photos are still under review, so the list below stays empty. Empty is not zero sales. The sales are counted above.
