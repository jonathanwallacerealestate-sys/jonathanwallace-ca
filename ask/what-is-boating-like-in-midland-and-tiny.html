What is boating like in Midland and Tiny Township?

Jonathan Wallace, REALTOR® · 0:49 video

The short answer

Some of the best boating Ontario has to offer is right at your doorstep. Jonathan Wallace's first season at Wye Heritage Marina came with a pool, brand new pickleball courts, a dog park and a friendly marina community, and his favourite part was calm, sunny afternoons anchored off Beckwith Island.

What Jonathan Wallace said

Our boat came out of the water this week, and I'm absolutely so grateful. This was our first year at Wye Heritage, and it was absolutely amazing. The people here were friendly. We used the pool, we used the brand new pickleball courts, the dog park. We had fish and chips at Finn City. We even hosted the GBGH Gala in one of their hangars, and it was an absolutely beautiful venue.

But my favourite part was afternoons anchored at Beckwith Island. The sun was out, the water was calm. That was my summer.

Now, living in Midland and Tiny Township gives you some of the best boating Ontario has to offer. So if you've been thinking about getting on the water, it's right at your doorstep. Thank you to the marina team, our whole boating community, and all of our new friends on O dock. Spring can't come soon enough. See you on the water next year.
