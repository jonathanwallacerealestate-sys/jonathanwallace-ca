About this home

Why this one stands out

If you've been dreaming about space, this is it. You get 8.5 acres overlooking the Sturgeon River, wrapped in mature forest, with trails to wander and a whole lot of quiet.

There are six bedrooms in total. The updated main bungalow has 2 + 2 bedrooms and two full bathrooms, and the garage and loft building adds two more bedrooms, a full bathroom, and a powder room.

That building went up in 2023, so it feels fresh and modern. Above the triple-car garage is a 1,449 sq. ft. accessory dwelling with radiant in-floor heat, vaulted ceilings, a great room, a semi-ensuite bathroom, and a large covered deck looking out over the property. It's a great setup for family or guests.

Back in the main house, there's a finished walkout basement and easy access to a big deck. The wood-burning furnace keeps heating efficient, and the owned MicroFIT solar panels bring in some extra revenue.

You're right beside the Simcoe County Forest, and Highway 400 is a short drive away. Honestly, you have to walk this one to really get it, and I'd be glad to show you around. 3,329 sq. ft. above grade plus a finished basement.
