Getting ready to sell in spring

Rural and cottage paperwork

By Jonathan Wallace, Realtor with Faris Team Real Estate Brokerage

What's my home worth?
Well tests, WETT, survey, permits and road agreements. Some take weeks.

Test your well water. Simcoe Muskoka District Health Unit provides free bacteria testing. Pick up bottles at the Midland office, and the sample has to be tested within 48 hours (SMDHU). You'll test again after the spring melt.

Book a WETT inspection for any wood stove, insert or pellet stove. Many insurers require one, and if a buyer can't get insurance, they can't get financing (WETT Inc.).

Start any shoreline approvals now. Docks, retaining walls and beach work can need Township, conservation authority or other approvals, and none of them happen fast. See Tiny's shoreline construction page and the NVCA permit FAQ.

Find your survey. If it's old or doesn't show your current fences, additions or outbuildings, talk to me about whether an updated Surveyor's Real Property Report makes sense (AOLS).

Order record searches. In Tiny, you can order a septic record search for your permit history (Tiny). The ESA can tell you if there are open electrical notifications on the property, and its full history requests take about 15 business days (ESA).

Oil tank? Find its age, location and inspection records. Older tanks and tanks of unknown age can create insurance and legal issues, so get the facts from TSSA or a registered contractor early (O. Reg. 213/01).

Private road or right-of-way? Gather any road-maintenance or snowplowing agreements. Buyers and lenders will want to know how year-round access works.

Cottage and rural checklist

  • Septic pumped and inspected, report on file; leaching bed clear of vehicles, patios and fire pits (Tiny)
  • Recent well water results
  • WETT certificate for any wood stove
  • Survey, shoreline and dock permits on file
  • Road or snowplowing agreements if you're on a private road

Quick answer

What extra paperwork do I need to sell a cottage or rural home on Georgian Bay?

Usually a septic pump-out report and permit history, a recent well water test, a WETT inspection for any wood stove, information on any oil tank, your survey, and any shoreline or dock permits. If you have private-road access, you'll need your road or snowplowing agreement too. Start gathering these in the fall. Some take weeks.
