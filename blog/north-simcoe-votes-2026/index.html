2026 municipal election, voter guide

North Simcoe Votes 2026

Election Day: Monday, October 26, 2026

This is a non-partisan resource guide to the council races in the Town of Midland, the Town of Penetanguishene, the Township of Tiny, the Township of Tay and the Town of Wasaga Beach. It sets out how to vote in each municipality and who is running for each office. It does not endorse any candidate.

Before you vote

How to vote in your municipality

Each municipality sets its own voting method. Check that you are on the voters' list before voting opens. Tiny's online registration closes Monday, October 12.

No paper ballots.
