About this home

Why this one stands out

This is the kind of place where you put the coffee on and just look out the window for a while. You've got water views, beach access at Corrie Hamelin Park just moments away, and the trails and beaches of Awenda Provincial Park a short drive from your door.

The bungalow has been beautifully maintained, with a well-appointed kitchen, custom built-ins throughout, and everything on one level. It sits on a private half-acre lot surrounded by nature.

The detached shop is a big deal. There's room for a workshop, recreational vehicles, storage, or the projects you never have space for in town. If you're a hobbyist or in the trades, you'll get it right away.

Two wood stoves keep the place warm and welcoming, so it works as a full-time home or a four-season Georgian Bay retreat.

And the privacy is the real thing. There are no close neighbours, yet the beaches, parks, and trails are all close by. 1,776 sq. ft. plus an unfinished basement.
