MLS® description

In the listing's words

Top 5 Reasons You Will Love This Home:

1) Experience the ultimate waterfront lifestyle with 107' of pristine sandy shoreline, offering a clean, shallow waterfront ideal for swimming, family gatherings, and endless summer enjoyment with spectacular sunset views across the bay creating a breathtaking backdrop that can be enjoyed from the comfort of your own property

2) Designed for year-round living and entertaining, this spacious four-season waterfront home features four bedrooms, three bathrooms, a functional family-friendly layout, a large kitchen, and a dedicated games room, providing plenty of space for hosting guests and creating lasting memories

3) Meticulously cared for by its original owners, this property showcases true pride of ownership and has never been used as a rental, with thoughtful maintenance and upgraded mechanical systems offering added confidence and peace of mind for years to come

4) Rarely found on waterfront properties, the exceptional parking and storage options include an attached single-car garage, a detached double-car garage, and ample driveway space, easily accommodating vehicles, boats, trailers, recreational equipment, and workshop needs

5) Ideally situated just minutes from Awenda Provincial Park, you'll enjoy convenient access to hiking trails, cycling routes, beaches, and nature adventures, while benefiting from year-round municipal road access, high-speed internet, natural gas service, a Generac backup generator, and a welcoming waterfront community. 2,287 above grade sq.ft. plus a finished basement.
