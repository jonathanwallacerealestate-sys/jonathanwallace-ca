Straight Answers · Selling

How do I help my parents plan a move when they don't know where to start?

Jonathan Wallace, REALTOR® · 0:51 video

The short answer

Start with a strategy session: no commitment to sell, just a conversation about what the home could sell for and what is worth doing before a move. Heading into winter there is time to make a plan, a few indoor repairs and some decluttering, one manageable job at a time, and just as important, what they don't need to spend time or money on. Instead of one overwhelming move, they get a clear checklist and decide on their own time.
