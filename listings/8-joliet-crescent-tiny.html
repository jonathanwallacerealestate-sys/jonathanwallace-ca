About this home

Why this one stands out

Just over half an acre, fully renovated, and steps from the beach. That's the short version. Here's the rest.

Outside, there's lots of parking, room for the boat and trailer, an enclosed dog run, a front patio, and a big, beautiful backyard. The garage has high ceilings with room for a lift, and the new roof and upgraded insulation mean fewer things to think about.

Inside, the main floor is bright and modern, with laminate through the living areas and bedrooms and ceramic tile in the kitchen and bathrooms. The great room has high ceilings and an extra-large fireplace. The new kitchen has quartz counters, a triple sink, a big prep station, and extra-deep cabinets.

The bedrooms sit in their own wing, away from the living space. The primary suite has a built-in closet and a private three-piece ensuite, and the other two bedrooms share a renovated four-piece bathroom.

Downstairs is fully finished, with big windows, a family room with both a fireplace and an enclosed wood stove, and a walkout, which makes it a great fit for in-laws. There's also a spa room with a Jacuzzi tub and a drop-down TV that could become a gym or wellness space, plus laundry, storage, room for a workshop, high-end water systems, and a camera system.

And then there's the location. You're steps from five private beaches with sandy shoreline and safe, inviting water, and nearby parks for picnics and afternoons with the grandkids. 1,762 sq. ft. above grade plus a finished basement.
