In the listing's words

Top 5 Reasons You Will Love This Home:

1) Tucked away on an extraordinary 50-acre wooded estate, this remarkable property is set well back from the road, offering an unmatched sense of seclusion, tranquility, and connection to nature

2) Designed to impress, the resort-inspired backyard showcases a stunning in-ground pool surrounded by beautiful stone landscaping, complete with a waterfall feature, heater, outdoor shower, and cabana, creating the ultimate setting for entertaining and summer enjoyment

3) Rich in European-inspired charm, the home exudes warmth and character throughout, presenting timeless architectural details and inviting living spaces that feel both elegant and welcoming

4) Enjoy the best of both worlds with a peaceful rural setting that feels miles away from it all, while remaining just minutes from the shops, restaurants, amenities, and waterfront attractions of downtown Midland

5) Surrounded by nature and endless opportunities for adventure, this exceptional property is perfectly suited for outdoor enthusiasts seeking year-round recreation, scenic trails, and a welcoming community atmosphere. 2,161 above grade sq.ft. plus a finished basement.
