2026 North Simcoe Municipal Election Results

By Jonathan Wallace, REALTOR®, Faris Team Real Estate Brokerage · October 27, 2026 · ~6 min read

Like this article? Join the weekly newsletter

Election Day was Monday, October 26, 2026. For some of you there will be excitement. For some there will be commiseration. That is normal. The people have spoken, and they exercised their right to vote.

Let's hope every municipality finds a way to make progress in challenging times. Let's hope the people who were elected work diligently for the people who elected them, and follow the platforms they ran on, without changing course and leaving voters feeling hoodwinked. Good elected officials stick to their mandates.

This is a non-partisan list of who won a council seat in Midland, Penetanguishene, Tay, Tiny and Wasaga Beach. It does not endorse anyone. Before the vote, I published a guide to the candidates: North Simcoe Votes 2026.

Acclaimed means no one else ran for that office. Returning means the person already held that office. New means they are new to that office. A count is official only once that municipality's clerk certifies it.

Key takeaways
  • Election Day was Monday, October 26, 2026. Voting closed at 8 p.m.
  • Tay Township and the Township of Tiny have published certified results. The Town of Midland says its clerk certified the count on October 27. Penetanguishene and Wasaga Beach figures below are the unofficial totals those towns had posted.
  • Acclaimed means no one else ran for that office. Returning means the person already held that office. New means they are new to that office.
  • A count is official only once that municipality's clerk certifies it. School board races are not in this roundup.

Certified October 27

Town of Midland

The Town of Midland says the clerk certified these results on October 27. The figures below are the ones the Town posted. Turnout on that posting was 33.1 per cent, from 5,400 ballots out of 16,314 eligible voters.

Mayor

Bill Gordon

Returning. 4,006 votes.

Deputy mayor

Beth Prost

Returning. 2,709 votes.

Councillors

Seven seats, in the order of the Town's posted count.

  • Blain Dusome, New. 2,643 votes.
  • Cody Oschefski, New. 2,628 votes.
  • Sheldon East, Returning. 2,622 votes.
  • Bill Meridis, Returning. 2,464 votes.
  • Jamie-Lee Ball, Returning. 2,401 votes.
  • Margaret Hamelin, New. 2,358 votes.
  • Jaz Patel, Returning. 2,135 votes.

Unofficial as of October 27

Town of Penetanguishene

As of October 27, the Town's results page still labelled these figures unofficial, and said certified results would be posted once the clerk finished them. Council is a mayor, a deputy mayor and five councillors. The five councillor seats below are the five highest totals on that page.

Mayor

Douglas Rawson

Acclaimed. No vote was held. The Town's results page lists him as Douglas Rawson.

Deputy mayor

Suzanne Marchand

New to the deputy mayor's role. 1,801 votes.

Councillors

  • Jill St. Amant, New. 2,017 votes.
  • Douglas R. Leroux, Returning. 1,868 votes.
  • Bonita Desroches, Returning. 1,602 votes.
  • Daryl O'Shea, New. 1,523 votes.
  • George Vadeboncoeur, Returning. 1,385 votes.

Certified

Tay Township

Tay Township says the clerk has certified the 2026 results. The mayor and deputy mayor were acclaimed on August 24, 2026. The township named the five councillors and did not publish vote totals for those seats, so none are listed here.

Mayor

Acclaimed. No vote was held.

Deputy mayor

Barry Norris

Acclaimed. No vote was held.

Councillors

All five are returning, in the order the township listed them.

  • Paul Raymond, Returning.

Certified October 27

Township of Tiny

Clerk and returning officer Tina Arbuckle certified these results on October 27. The certificate records turnout of 45.23 per cent, from 8,889 ballots out of 19,651 eligible voters. Council is a mayor, a deputy mayor and three councillors.

Mayor

Dave Brunelle

New. 3,515 votes.

Deputy mayor

Karen Zulynik

New. 3,869 votes.

Councillors

  • Jessie Garland, New. 3,786 votes.
  • Don Anthony, New. 3,487 votes.
  • Steffen Walma, Returning. 2,371 votes.

Unofficial as of October 27

Town of Wasaga Beach

Wasaga Beach had not posted a clerk's certificate when this was written. The Town said the clerk would certify results on October 27, and its elections page still called the count unofficial. The figures below are the unofficial totals on the Town's results portal. CollingwoodToday reported the same totals and the same winners. Council is a mayor, a deputy mayor and five councillors.

Mayor

Brian Smith

Returning. 6,102 votes.

Deputy mayor

Mike Foley

New. 6,107 votes.

Councillors

  • Kyle Graves, New. 6,785 votes.
  • Sasha-Rose Dileo, Returning. 5,832 votes.
  • Joe Belanger, Returning. 5,700 votes.
  • Ellen Timms, Returning. 5,499 votes. The Town's certified candidate list and results portal spell her name Timms.
  • Richard White, Returning. 5,251 votes.
