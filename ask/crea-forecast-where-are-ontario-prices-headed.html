What Jonathan Wallace said

CREA just updated its housing forecast, and honestly, it lines up with what I've been seeing on the ground. It's probably going to get a little bit worse before it gets better. They're now expecting Ontario home prices to finish the year down 3%.

Now here's why. Fixed-rate mortgages are on the rise, and the big banks and investors are betting that the Bank of Canada will raise its rates over the next year to get inflation under control. So borrowing is about to get more expensive, not less.

And on the ground, buyers are coming off the sidelines, but the deals happening right now lean in the buyer's favour. Sellers aren't getting their dream number. They're getting the price that they can stomach. However, CREA does expect things to pick up in 2027. So if you're buying, this could be your window. If you're selling, let's price it right from day one. What do you think's going to happen next with pricing?
