About this property

Room to build, close to the sand

If you've been waiting for the right spot to build in Tiny, pull up a chair. This is a 74' x 320' lot between Ronald Avenue and Charles Avenue, near Tiny Beaches Road South, with plenty of room for a full-time home or a cottage you keep in the family for years.

It sits between Edmore Beach and Deanlea Beach, and the soft white sand is a short walk away. Evening walks to catch the sunset become part of the routine pretty quickly out here.

The street is lined with established homes and mature trees, so you get a real neighbourhood feel and still keep your privacy.

A lot of the early homework is already done. Natural gas and high-speed internet are available, the septic design is complete, and the well due diligence has been approved by the township. That takes a good chunk of the guesswork out of planning a build.

Wasaga Beach, Elmvale, and Midland are all close for groceries, dinners out, and everyday errands. If you want to walk the lot with me, book a time or send me a note. Bring your sketches.
