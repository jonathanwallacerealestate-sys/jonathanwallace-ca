Simcoe County Real Estate Market History: Home Sales and Prices, 2021 to 2026

Market history·Updated with October 2026 figures·~4 min read
Thinking of selling? Book a home evaluation

By Jonathan Wallace, REALTOR® · Updated with October 2026 figures

Simcoe County recorded 7,703 home sales in 2025 at an average sale price of $790,637, down from 21,333 sales in 2021. Through October 2026, 6,541 homes had sold at an average of $738,300. In October 2026 the market had 7.69 months of inventory, which makes it a buyer's market.

Simcoe County figures are the board's county-wide totals. The town markets on this site cover different areas and do not add up to the county total. Full years are January to December. 2026 is January to October, the latest month reported.

What changed over five years

Simcoe County sold 21,333 homes in 2021 and 7,703 in 2025, down 63.9 percent. New listings did not follow sales down. They were 27,256 in 2021 and 26,081 in 2025, which is why inventory has built up and the county has been a buyer's market since early 2025.

The average sale price peaked at $895,111 in 2022. By 2025 it was $790,637, slightly below 2021's $810,210. Homes sold at 103.5 percent of asking on average in 2021 and at 97.1 percent in 2025, and took 42 days instead of 16. Through October 2026 the county had 6,541 sales against 6,728 over the same months of 2025.

Common questions about the Simcoe County market

How many homes sold in Simcoe County in 2025?

7,703 homes sold in Simcoe County in 2025, compared with 9,258 in 2024 and 21,333 in 2021.

What is the average home price in Simcoe County?

The average sale price was $790,637 for 2025 and $738,300 for January to October 2026. An average reflects which homes sold, not the value of any one home.

Is Simcoe County a buyer's market right now?

In October 2026, Simcoe County had 7.69 months of inventory, which is a buyer's market. Above six months is a buyer's market.

How long do homes take to sell in Simcoe County?

Homes that sold in 2025 averaged 42 days on market. In October 2026 the average was 42 days. When a listing is cancelled and put back on the market, the days on market count starts over, so some homes have been for sale longer than the number shows.

Are home sales in Simcoe County up or down in 2026?

Through October 2026, 6,541 homes sold, compared with 6,728 over the same ten months of 2025, down 2.8 percent.
