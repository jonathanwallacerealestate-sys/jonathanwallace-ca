About this home

Why this one stands out

This one is all about beach life. The home comes with private beach access, and the sunsets over the water are the kind you stop what you're doing to watch. Deanlea Beach is about a 10 minute walk from the door, too.

The lot is big and full of tall, mature trees, and a private, winding path leads right to the shoreline, so a swim or a walk on the sand is never far away.

Inside the raised bungalow, the main floor is bright and open with soaring vaulted ceilings. Outside, the brick and stone exterior and a wraparound porch give you a great place to sit and take it all in.

Downstairs, the partially finished rec room has 8.5' ceilings and a bathroom with a walk-in shower. Home gym, studio, or movie nights: you get to decide.

There's a garden shed and plenty of parking for guests. Midland, Wasaga Beach, and Elmvale are all close, and Toronto is a comfortable drive when you need it. 1,257 sq. ft. above grade plus a partially finished basement.
