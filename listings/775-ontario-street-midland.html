About this home

Why this one stands out

Let's start in the kitchen, because that's where everyone ends up anyway. It's brand new (2026), full of natural light, with clean modern finishes, stainless steel appliances, and an induction range.

Then head out back. The yard backs onto forest, so it's quiet and private. There's a dedicated hot tub area, an outdoor fireplace lounge, and the original Little Lake Cabin bunkie, which is a lot of fun. A 6' x 12' front deck and a 12' x 16' rear deck give you two great spots to eat outside or just sit for a while.

The 24' x 24' double garage is fully powered, with room for vehicles, toys, tools, and everything else.

The list of updates is long, and that's a good thing: new windows (2023), exterior insulation (2023), house roof (2024), garage and bunkie roof (2025), landscaping (2025), hot water tank (2025), and new decks (2026).

You're minutes from the hospital, the marinas, downtown shops and restaurants, and Midland's parks and waterfront trails. 899 sq. ft. above grade plus a partially finished basement.
