What this is

An opinion of value, not an appraisal

In my opinion, the market value of 188 Old Mill Road, Port Severn, is between $2,000,000 and $2,200,000 as of November 5, 2026. Market value here means the most probable price, on that date, after reasonable exposure in a competitive market, with the buyer and seller each acting prudently and for their own interest, and with neither under undue duress.

I have no interest in the property, present or contemplated. This opinion does not depend on the value reported. The facts I relied on are true and correct to the best of my knowledge. I am not responsible for matters that are legal in character. Title is assumed to be good. This report does not require me to give court testimony.

Sold prices and listing history are as reported on MLS®. Information is deemed reliable but not guaranteed. The one-page Sold Comparables PDF supports the letter. It is not itself an appraisal.
