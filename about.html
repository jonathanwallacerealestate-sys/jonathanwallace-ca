My story

Local roots, a real approach.

I'm a full-time REALTOR® with Faris Team Real Estate, Brokerage. This isn't a territory I cover, it's home. I live in Tiny Township, and I work from Midland across Penetanguishene, Tay and Wasaga Beach.

Because I understand this area and the life it offers, I can paint a real picture of what it's like to live here. For sellers, that means marketing your home around the life it comes with: the water, the towns, the schools and the shoreline, so buyers see that, not just the floor plan. For buyers, it means helping you weigh the real value of moving into the area, not just the house. I market a home the way buyers search today, and I price, position and negotiate to the result you're after.

The work has been recognized: I was named Best REALTOR® in the 2025 Midland Readers' Choice Awards, and I am the number one rated agent for Midland and Penetanguishene on RateMyAgent, with a 4.96 star average from 16 verified reviews. When I'm not on a listing, you'll find me out on Georgian Bay or down on the dock at Wye Heritage with my partner Courtney and our two dogs, and giving back through the local causes below.

Giving back

Invested in the people who show up

I've always been drawn to the people who give their time to make a place better. Here at home, that means supporting the service groups, volunteers and local organizations who keep our communities strong. Beyond our own shores, it means taking part in international service missions that support families and communities who need it most. I've been recognized by Rotary for my contributions to the organization.
