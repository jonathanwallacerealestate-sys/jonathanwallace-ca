# Letter of Opinion client email template

From [Your Name], REALTOR®, [Your Brokerage].

When to use: after the letter is a one-page PDF. The letter is addressed to everyone on title. This email goes only to the person who asked. Paste it into your own email. You send it.

![Sample email for Jordan Sample about 123 Banana Street. Fictional.](examples/email.png)

Give this file to your AI with the Letter of Opinion kit. The opinion of value is yours. The AI only writes the note.

## Rules

- Short paragraphs. A blank line between paragraphs. Never one solid block.
- No em dashes. Canadian spelling (neighbourhood, honour, colour).
- Do not invent a comp, a price, a date, or a name.
- If you are replying on an existing thread, keep that subject.
- Otherwise the subject is: Letter of Opinion - [street address].
- Omit the maximum list price if you did not set one.
- A purpose line only if you named a purpose. It does not change the letter.
- If the client asked whether an earlier assessment is still current, and you gave the earlier figures, you may say the earlier assessment is no longer current. If you did not give earlier figures, do not invent a "what changed" section.

## Shape

```
Hi [First name],

[One or two sentences. Say that the Letter of Opinion is attached.]

Opinion of value, as of [date]

- Current market value range: $[low] to $[high]
- Maximum recommended list price: $[max]

[Two or three sentences. Name each comp you supplied and the price. Say which are sold and which are active. Add nothing.]

[Purpose line, only if you gave a purpose.]

If you would like to talk through what this means for your options, I am happy to walk you through it.

Kindly,

[Your Name]
REALTOR®, [Your Brokerage]
[phone]
[email]
```
