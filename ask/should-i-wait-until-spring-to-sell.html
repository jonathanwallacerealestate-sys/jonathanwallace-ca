Straight Answers · Selling

Should I wait until spring to sell my house in Midland?

Jonathan Wallace, REALTOR® · 1:07 video

The short answer

Waiting for spring feels safer, but it can cost more than you think when prices are trending down. The question worth asking is simple: if prices are lower in the spring, does that change your plans? Nobody has a crystal ball, but answering that honestly tells you what really matters.

What Jonathan Wallace said

Waiting until spring feels safe, but it could cost you more than you think. Now, let me tell you about Tom and Linda. Thirty years in their Midland home. Their kids' heights are still etched on the kitchen doorframe. Now the stairs are getting harder, and they're ready for something smaller and closer to their grandchildren.

They tell me, well, wait until the spring, my garden shows better. And I understand. It's a big step, and spring does feel safer. But right now, prices are trending in the wrong direction. So I asked them one honest question: what if the prices are lower in the spring? Will that change your plans for the future, or is that something you're going to be okay with? And they both went quiet, because nobody has a crystal ball. But that one question helped them decide what really mattered.

Now, no real estate agent wants to sell a home for less. Most of us got into this business because we care about people, and a lot of how we feel about our work comes from how we do for our clients. So I'd rather ask the hard question today than have them find out in six months. So if you have been thinking about waiting, let's sit down and talk through what it could mean for you.
