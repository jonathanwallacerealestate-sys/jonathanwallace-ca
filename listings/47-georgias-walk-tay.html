About this home

Why this one stands out

Here's the part I keep coming back to: the backyard backs right onto the Tay Trail. Walk, bike, or take the dog out the back and just keep going.

It's in a neighbourhood people ask me about all the time, with schools, parks, and green space all within walking distance.

Out back, the forest gives you real privacy. The walkout basement opens to a yard full of mature trees, and the gardens bring colour all season long with daylilies, tiger lilies, hostas, ferns, and roses.

The kitchen has stainless steel appliances, a gas range, and a built-in microwave, with room for everyone to gather while you cook.

The layout works for real life, with a bright family room, a home office, and a second bedroom that opens to a private upper deck. 1,032 sq. ft. above grade plus a finished basement. Come take a look, and bring your walking shoes.
