Wasaga Beach Real Estate Market History: Home Sales and Prices, 2021 to 2026

By Jonathan Wallace, REALTOR® · Updated with October 2026 figures

Wasaga Beach recorded 546 home sales in 2025 at an average sale price of $658,363, down from 1,163 sales in 2021. Through October 2026, 493 homes had sold at an average of $672,279. In October 2026 the market had 8.85 months of inventory, which makes it a buyer's market.

The board reports Wasaga Beach as its own market. Full years are January to December. 2026 is January to October, the latest month reported.

What changed over five years

Wasaga Beach sold 1,163 homes in 2021 and 546 in 2025, down 53.1 percent. Unlike most of the region, supply grew for years after the peak. New listings went from 1,458 in 2021 to 2,275 in 2024, which is why Wasaga has spent much of the past two years with more homes for sale than buyers.

The average sale price peaked at $774,255 in 2022 and was $658,363 in 2025, below where it was in 2021. Through October 2026, Wasaga had 493 sales against 487 over the same months of 2025, one of three markets on this site running ahead of last year.

Common questions about the Wasaga Beach market

How many homes sold in Wasaga Beach in 2025?

546 homes sold in Wasaga Beach in 2025, compared with 759 in 2024 and 1,163 in 2021.

What is the average home price in Wasaga Beach?

The average sale price was $658,363 for 2025 and $672,279 for January to October 2026. An average reflects which homes sold, not the value of any one home.

Is Wasaga Beach a buyer's market right now?

In October 2026, Wasaga Beach had 8.85 months of inventory, which is a buyer's market. Above six months is a buyer's market.

How long do homes take to sell in Wasaga Beach?

Homes that sold in 2025 averaged 50 days on market. In October 2026 the average was 51 days. When a listing is cancelled and put back on the market, the days on market count starts over, so some homes have been for sale longer than the number shows.

Are home sales in Wasaga Beach up or down in 2026?

Through October 2026, 493 homes sold, compared with 487 over the same ten months of 2025, up 1.2 percent.
