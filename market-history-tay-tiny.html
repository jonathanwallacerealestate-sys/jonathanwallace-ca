Tay Township and Tiny Township Real Estate Market History: Home Sales and Prices, 2021 to 2026

Market history·Updated with October 2026 figures·~4 min read

By Jonathan Wallace, REALTOR® · Updated with October 2026 figures

Tay Township and Tiny Township recorded 416 home sales in 2025 at an average sale price of $744,470, down from 1,483 sales in 2021. Through October 2026, 371 homes had sold at an average of $699,052. In October 2026 the market had 10.87 months of inventory, which makes it a buyer's market.

The board reports Tay Township and Tiny Township together as one market. These figures cover both townships, including Victoria Harbour, Port McNicoll, Waubaushene, Perkinsfield, Lafontaine and the Tiny shoreline. Full years are January to December. 2026 is January to October, the latest month reported.

What changed over five years

Tay and Tiny has seen the biggest drop from the 2021 peak of the markets on this site. 1,483 homes sold in 2021. In 2025, 416 did, down 71.9 percent. New listings barely changed over the same years, 2,048 in 2021 and 1,696 in 2025.

Prices held up better than sales. The average sale price peaked at $775,109 in 2022 and was $744,470 in 2025, still above 2021's $685,978. What changed most was time. Homes took 30 days to sell in 2021 and 54 days in 2025. Through October 2026, sales were running slightly ahead of 2025, at 371 against 362.

Common questions about the Tay Township and Tiny Township market

How many homes sold in Tay Township and Tiny Township in 2025?

416 homes sold in Tay Township and Tiny Township in 2025, compared with 603 in 2024 and 1,483 in 2021.

What is the average home price in Tay Township and Tiny Township?

The average sale price was $744,470 for 2025 and $699,052 for January to October 2026. An average reflects which homes sold, not the value of any one home.

Is Tay Township and Tiny Township a buyer's market right now?

In October 2026, Tay Township and Tiny Township had 10.87 months of inventory, which is a buyer's market. Above six months is a buyer's market.

How long do homes take to sell in Tay Township and Tiny Township?

Homes that sold in 2025 averaged 54 days on market. In October 2026 the average was 46 days. When a listing is cancelled and put back on the market, the days on market count starts over, so some homes have been for sale longer than the number shows.

Are home sales in Tay Township and Tiny Township up or down in 2026?

Through October 2026, 371 homes sold, compared with 362 over the same ten months of 2025, up 2.5 percent.
