What Jonathan Wallace said

More than 1,100 new homes are planned for Penetang, and that's no accident.

I sit at kitchen tables all the time with people who raised their families here. The house is too big now, and they want to stay in town, but there's been nowhere to go. Well, that's about to change. There's a real mix coming: apartments, townhouses and detached homes. Some are years away, but some could start any day now.

And when those people finally move, their family homes open up for the next young family. If you own an older detached home, it's worth knowing: buyers are about to get a lot more choice.

So why Penetang? From what I see on the ground, it's the most progressive town around here. For me and the people I work with, it's been the easiest to deal with. They have a clear vision, a simple process, and a council and staff who actually work with you.

The town even cut the fees builders pay by 60% for the next three years. So the builders I deal with have chosen to invest right here. Other towns could learn a lot from Penetang, and kudos to them.
