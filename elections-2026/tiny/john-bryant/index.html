Candidate, Township of Tiny

John Bryant

Candidate for CouncillorTownship of Tiny3 seats, elected at large

Tiny resident since 2016, landscape architect and municipal manager of more than 35 years (Vaughan, Barrie, Milton and Orillia), running on "Leadership that listens."

Priorities

Source: Candidate's own website or campaign materials. Up to five points.

  1. Respect taxpayers: manage taxes and spending responsibly so residents get good value
  2. Plan with care on the new Town Hall and take a long-term view of septage management
  3. Clarify waterfront rights: respect lawful property and access rights and seek negotiated, practical solutions
  4. Support off-leash dog parks throughout Tiny
