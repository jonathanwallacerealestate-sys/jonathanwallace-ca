Questions

When is Election Day?

Election Day is Monday, October 26, 2026. Voting closes at 8 p.m.

How do I vote in Tay?

Internet and telephone only. In-person help is available at Voter Assistance Centres. There is no paper ballot.

When can I vote online or by phone?

Voting runs from Tuesday, October 13 at 10 a.m. to Monday, October 26 at 8 p.m.

How do I check I'm on the voters' list?

The Township election page explains how to confirm or update your voters' list information. Check you are registered.

What do I need to vote?

You need to be on the voters' list. Voting is by internet and telephone, with no paper ballot.

Where do I get help?

Township of Tay's election page has voter help and who to contact. Official election page.
