Candidate, Township of Tiny

Chuck Stradling

Candidate for MayorTownship of Tiny1 seat, elected at large

Tiny resident for more than 15 years who had a 31-year career with GO Transit and senior leadership at BOMA Toronto, and has been involved with the Lafontaine Area Shoreline Homeowners Association since 2015, according to the campaign site. The site says the campaign is not part of a slate.

Priorities

Source: Candidate's own website or campaign materials. Up to five points.

  1. Full transparency and accountability, including fewer closed-door sessions
  2. Fiscal responsibility, with annual tax increases held to no more than the rise in the cost of living where reasonably possible
  3. More compromise and less litigation
  4. Town hall meetings on major undertakings and a strategic plan shaped by residents
  5. Protect the environment and build better relations with neighbouring municipalities and Simcoe County
